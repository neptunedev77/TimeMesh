## Why TimeMesh?

Getting a group of people in the same place at the same time shouldn't take a dozen messages, a few screenshots, and a poll nobody answers.

TimeMesh brings everyone's availability together and turns it into a clear picture of when your group can actually meet, whether that's for a project, a meeting, or a night out.

## What it's about

| | |
|:--|:--|
| 🔗 **Easy to join** | Bring people in with a simple link. |
| 🗓️ **Shared availability** | See your group's time in one place. |
| 📆 **Beyond a single week** | Plan ahead across as many weeks as you need. |
| 🤝 **Flexible by design** | Find the best moments, even when not everyone can make it. |
